The five-stage research cycle, ending with the idea that research happens all around us.

1

Ask a question

Start with a clear, focused question worth investigating.

2

Review the literature

Find out what's already known before adding to it.

3

Design the study

Plan a method that can actually answer the question.

4

Collect & analyze data

Gather evidence systematically and make sense of it.

5

Communicate findings

Share results so others can build on or challenge them.

Research is all around us

Not just labs — it happens in kitchens, gyms, classrooms, and on our phones.
